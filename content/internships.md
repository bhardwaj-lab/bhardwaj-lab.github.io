## Master's internships

Are you a student enrolled in a master's program in (applied) data science / computer science or a related field, and interested in gaining experience in bioinformatics? Or are you enrolled in a bioinformatics/life science program and would like to do a research project related to computational genomics? We have **multiple projects suitable for a master's internship/thesis!**

Please consider the following before applying:

 - We can only offer internships that involve a **minimum of 6 months of research work**.
 - We do not offer funded internship. Students are welcome to bring in independent funding to support their internship.
 - Students must already bring **solid programming skills** in a language suitable for bioinformatics, specifically (at least one of) Python, R, C/C++, Rust. Additionally, linux/bash skills and experience with high-performance computing are desirable. **Vibe coding** skills do not count.
 - For the students with **technical/quantitative background**, the knowledge of biology is not required. We expect you to bring in good foundation in data science and/or algorithms. An interest in learning the biological background behind our project (particularly the data generation process) is desirable.
 - For students with **life science background**, the knowledge of data science or algorithms is not necessary. We expect you to bring in a solid understanding of molecular biology and (regulatory) genomics. An interest in learning the statistical and algorithmic foundation of data analysis is desirable.

For students enrolled in Utrecht University's **MCLS or CSnD master's programs**, we also have specific opportunities for projects that **combine experiments with bioinformatics**. Feel free to clarify this in the email if you are interested in such projects. Prior experience working in a molecular biology lab, together with basic programming skills in Python or R shall be sufficient for such projects.

### Application process

**Step-1:** For the application, send us your CV, along with the information below:
  - A list of relevant courses and grades.
  - Link to an online code repository (github/gitlab) showcasing your programming experience. If that's not available, send us a report of a practical assignment which highlights your programming and (optionally) your data science skills.
  - A short motivation statement about your research interests.
  - The expected start and end date of your internship, and a link/document form your master program that outlines the internship process.

If we have a spot available, we will require a reference letter from the supervisor of your current/previous internship based on a form which we will send to them. Please confirm with them in advance whether they can fill this form for us (takes 5-10 minutes) and add them in CC to your email. If that's not possible, let us know why. If you can not provide a referee's contact at step-1 and we still have a spot available, we will need a longer interview (below).

**Step-2:** We will schedule an interview (preferably on-site), which includes a short presentation from you (10 min) about your background and previous project experience, and a 1-on-1 discussion of potential projects that might be suitable and interesting for you.

**Step-3:** If you are selected, you can prepare a project pre-proposal/abstract based on literature search and the information provided by us. You (together with us) also identify an external examiner who, together with your daily supervisor from our lab, will read and approve your proposal. Once the proposal is approved by everyone, your internship can begin.

**In some master programs a detailed proposal is part of the project itself (or part of interim assessment). This is different from *step-3* above.**


## Bachelor's internships

We can support bachelor's thesis projects **for UU enrolled life science or biomedical science students** from time to time. You need a solid background in genomics/gene regulation, stemcells/developmental biology, and (advance) bioinformatics for our projects. Contact the thesis coordinator of your program for details. We do not support students from other programs/universities for bachelor's thesis.
